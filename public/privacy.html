Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information LaunchNestAI collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with. LaunchNestAI is operated by Kim Donnelly. By using LaunchNestAI, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. What we collect

InformationWhy
Your email addressTo sign you in (passwordless, one-time code) and to contact you about your account
Your Etsy order numberTo verify your purchase of LaunchNestAI through Etsy
Your Etsy shop and listing data (shop name, listing titles, descriptions, prices, tags, materials, photos)Read via the Etsy API, scoped to your own shop, so you can select a listing and generate a landing page for it
Generated landing page contentStored so your landing page stays available at its link and you can view/manage it later

We only access Etsy data for the shop linked to your account — LaunchNestAI enforces that you can't view or generate pages for a shop that isn't yours.

2. How your listing data is used to generate a page

When you create a landing page, your selected listing's text (title, description, price, tags, materials) and photos are sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which writes the page's marketing copy based only on that listing's real information. This happens per page, at the moment you request it — your data isn't used to train any AI model.

3. Who we share data with

We share data with the following service providers, only as needed to run LaunchNestAI:

  • Etsy — to read your shop/listing data and verify purchases, via Etsy's API
  • Supabase — our database and login provider, which stores your account and generated landing pages
  • Anthropic — to generate your landing page's marketing content from your listing data

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Cookies

LaunchNestAI uses only the essential cookies/local storage needed to keep you signed in. We don't use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

5. Data retention

We keep your account information and generated landing pages until you ask us to delete them or close your account. We keep a record of used Etsy order numbers to prevent the same purchase from being used to create multiple accounts.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time by contacting us (see below).

7. Children's privacy

LaunchNestAI is intended for Etsy sellers and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of LaunchNestAI after a change means you accept the updated policy.
